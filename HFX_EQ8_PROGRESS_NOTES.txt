HFX EQ8 DEVELOPMENT PATCH - 25 NOV 2026

This source update expands the HFX equalizer DSP and parameter model from five bands to eight bands.
Each band now has host-automatable Frequency, Gain, Q and Filter Type parameters.
The eight filter choices remain:
1. 48 dB/oct Low Cut
2. 12 dB/oct Low Cut
3. Low Shelf
4. Bell
5. Notch
6. High Shelf
7. 12 dB/oct High Cut
8. 48 dB/oct High Cut

The EQ editor now provides eight filter selectors and 24 rotary controls (Frequency, Gain, Q per band),
and the graph displays eight draggable band nodes.
The audio processing loop applies all eight EQ bands.

Preset and Auto Mix code paths were intentionally not redesigned. Existing preset logic still sets its
original five named EQ gains/frequencies; the additional bands retain their stored/default values.
